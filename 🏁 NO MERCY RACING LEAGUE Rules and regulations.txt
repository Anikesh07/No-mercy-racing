🏁 NO MERCY RACING LEAGUE
📜 OFFICIAL RULES & REGULATIONS (WITHOUT POINT SYSTEM)
📌 1. EVENT STRUCTURE

1.1 The event will run for 7 consecutive days.
1.2 Minimum 5 teams required to start the event.

1.3 Each team must consist of:

4 Main Racers
1 Reserve Racer
At least 1 female racer (mandatory)

1.4 All teams must register and receive admin approval before participation.
1.5 Participation confirms full acceptance of all rules.

💰 2. ENTRY & MATCH RULES

2.1 Entry Fee:

Each team must pay $25,000

2.2 Rivalry Clash (Challenge Race):

Both teams must pay $10,000 each
Winner takes full amount

2.3 No refunds under any circumstances.

🏎️ 3. DAILY MATCH FORMAT

Each day includes:

Team Grand Prix (Main Race)
Duo Clash (2 players per team)
Solo Showdown (1 player per team – compulsory)

Optional:

Rivalry Clash (team vs team challenge)
👥 4. PARTICIPATION RULES

4.1 Every team must participate in all scheduled races daily.

4.2 Player Restrictions:

Top performers in Team Grand Prix cannot participate in Duo Clash or Solo Showdown
Top performers in Duo Clash cannot participate in Solo Showdown
Players in Duo Clash cannot participate in Solo Showdown

4.3 Teams must follow correct player rotation and assignments.

⚠️ 5. DOMINANCE RULE

If all 4 racers of a team finish in Top 5:

Reserve player must participate in Duo Clash and Solo Showdown
Bottom 2 racers must participate in Duo Clash and Solo Showdown
Top racers are restricted from participating in these races
⚔️ 6. RIVALRY CLASH RULES

6.1 Teams can only challenge:

One team directly above OR below them in rankings

6.2 Each team has:

1 Defensive Pass (can refuse one challenge without penalty)

6.3 After using the pass:

Refusing a challenge is not allowed and will result in penalties

6.4 Once a challenge is accepted, it cannot be cancelled.

🏁 7. GRID & START RULES

7.1 Team Grand Prix uses a 2-line grid system

7.2 Day 1:

Grid is assigned randomly but fairly

7.3 Day 2 onwards:

Grid is based on reverse ranking

7.4 No jump start or early movement allowed
7.5 Drivers must maintain lane discipline at race start

🚫 8. DIRTY DRIVING RULE (STRICT)

The following are strictly prohibited:

Intentional ramming
PIT maneuvers
Forcing players off track
Brake-checking
Blocking to cause crash
Penalties:
Minor offense → Warning
Moderate offense → Position penalty
Major offense → Disqualification
Severe offense → Disqualification + further action
Repeat Offense:
Second offense → Race ban
Third offense → Event removal
🚫 9. DISCONNECT RULE
Disconnect during race = last position
No rejoin advantage allowed
Repeated disconnect may lead to penalties
❌ 10. REFUSAL TO PLAY
No team is allowed to skip any race

If a team refuses:

Heavy monetary penalty ($300,000 – $500,000)
Additional disciplinary action may be applied
⚠️ 11. WRONG PARTICIPATION
Using restricted or invalid player:
Player result will not be counted
Repeated violation:
Team may face penalties
🔄 12. PLAYER SWAP RULE
Maximum 2 swaps per team during event
Swaps must be declared before race
No mid-race swaps allowed
🎥 13. POV SUBMISSION RULE
Top players must submit race POV
Submission deadline: within 24 hours

Failure to submit:

Action will be taken by admin
⚖️ 14. TIE-BREAKER RULE

If teams are tied:

Each team selects their best driver
A 1v1 race is conducted
Hardest track will be used
Winner decides final position
👑 15. ADMIN AUTHORITY
Admin has full authority over:
Rule enforcement
Disputes
Final decisions
All decisions are final and binding
🧠 FINAL STATEMENT
Play fair
Follow rules
Respect decisions

👉 This event rewards discipline, teamwork, and consistency.
